# Paid pilot

The pilot is the launch gate, not a marketing afterthought. Do not charge the public until it passes.

## Cohort

- 15 macOS streamers who already post clips to TikTok/Reels/Shorts.
- Mix: 5 low-chat (<50 concurrent), 5 mid-size, 5 high-chat/raiders.
- At least 3 use OBS + a Mac daily; at least 3 have never used a CLI.
- Exclude anyone who cannot confirm permission to record the channels they monitor.

## Recruitment script

> I’m testing a Mac app that watches your Twitch chat, saves the moments chat reacts to, turns them into 9:16 clips, and can drop them in Dropbox or Google Drive. It runs while you stream. Would you try it for two weeks and tell me honestly whether it’s worth $39? I’ll help you install it and you keep the clips.

## Consent and privacy

- Send the EULA/privacy link before install.
- Get written permission to use anonymized metrics and optional quotes.
- Do not collect Twitch credentials; the app does not ask for them.
- Do not record a channel without the streamer’s permission. The onboarding checkbox is a reminder, not a legal release.
- Offer deletion of pilot data on request.

## Onboarding session (20–30 min)

1. Install the notarized DMG on a clean Mac; launch from Applications.
2. Complete Getting Started; choose Local only first.
3. Enter the channel and start capture; trigger a manual clip.
4. Open History; show local and remote paths.
5. Connect Dropbox or Google Drive; run one test upload.
6. Show license/trial screen and checkout link (do not force purchase during the trial).
7. Ask them to stream at least twice and post at least one clip.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Complete install + onboarding unaided | ≥10/15 | screen share / event |
| First clip within 30 min | ≥12/15 | history |
| Clips/week per active user | ≥5 | history |
| Upload success | ≥99% | sidecar/queue |
| Crash-free sessions | ≥99% | crash reports/logs |
| Trial → paid | ≥5 preorders or 10 paying users | Lemon Squeezy |
| Refund rate | <5% | Lemon Squeezy |
| Support tickets | <1/user/month | support inbox |
| Would be disappointed if unavailable | ≥40% | survey (Sean Ellis) |
| Willing to pay $39 | ≥8/15 | survey |

## Survey (day 14)

1. What did you try to do that failed?
2. Did the 9:16 framing save you time? Why/why not?
3. Which storage did you use? Did uploads ever fail?
4. What would make this a must-have?
5. How disappointed would you be if it disappeared? (very / somewhat / not)
6. Would you pay $39 one-time? $29? $49? Subscription?
7. What almost stopped you from finishing setup?
8. Can we quote your feedback?

## Go/no-go

Launch only if the launch gate in the commercial plan passes. If onboarding is the failure, fix setup before pricing. If clips are not good enough, fix framing/selection before charging. If uploads fail, stop and fix the queue. Do not interpret a small, friendly cohort as demand.
